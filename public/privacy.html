Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 November 2026

The short version. CelerySync keeps the information you put in so the app can work for you — your routine, your check-ins, your reminders. Some of it is health information, so we treat it with care. We never sell it, never share it with advertisers or supplement brands, and you can ask us for a copy or delete your account at any time.

Who we are

CelerySync ("we", "us") is an independent Australian app, based in Queensland, that helps you keep your own daily routine on track. It is not affiliated with Anthony William or Medical Medium. This policy explains how we handle personal information under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles.

Contact us about privacy at allij@live.com.au.

What we collect

  • Your account: your email address and password (stored securely by our login provider — we never see your password).
  • Your profile: the name you give, and optionally an age range, gender, and whether you'd like cycle tracking.
  • Your routine: the rhythm items, reminders, programs, templates and supplements you add, what you tick off and when, and the history of changes you make.
  • How you're feeling: check-ins you choose to log — energy, mood, mental clarity, symptoms in your own words, sleep, water, notes, and period dates if you turn on cycle tracking. This is health information, which the law treats as sensitive.
  • Wearables (only if you connect one): sleep, heart-rate and HRV readings from Oura or Apple Health.
  • Conversations with the companion: what you type in the chat and what you say to the voice companion (as text transcripts), and short notes the app keeps so the companion remembers your routine and preferences.
  • Payments: handled by Stripe. We receive your plan and payment status — never your card number.
  • Technical: your device's notification address (if you allow reminders), your time zone, and basic usage events (for example, which tab was opened) so we can see what's working.
  • People you invite: if you invite a carer or email a report to someone, we use the email address you give for that purpose only.

We only collect health information with your consent — by choosing to enter it, you consent to us storing and using it as described here. You can use the app without logging any health details.

How we use it

  • To run the app for you: show your day, send the reminders you set, keep your history, and produce the reports you ask for.
  • To let the voice and chat companion help you — for example, knowing what's in your rhythm and what you've already done today.
  • To take payments and manage your plan.
  • To keep the service secure, fix problems and improve it (using usage counts, not your health details).
  • To email you things you've asked for: reports, carer invitations, weekly summaries and account emails.

We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or share it with supplement brands or anyone else for marketing. Reports and summaries describe only what you logged — they are not medical assessments.

We also share your information with people you choose: a carer you invite (they can see your progress, read-only) or someone you email a report to. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • The separate log of voice conversations we keep to check the app is working properly is deleted automatically after 90 days.
  • Your conversation history (which lets the companion remember your routine) stays until you clear it — Companion tab → clear memory — or delete your account.
  • Everything else is kept while your account is open, so your history and reports keep working.
  • When you delete your account, your profiles, routine, check-ins and conversations are deleted. Payment records may be kept by Stripe and us where the law requires (for example, tax records).

Hume AI and Anthropic handle conversation data under their own privacy policies and may keep it for a limited time (for example, for safety monitoring).

Keeping it safe

Your data is sent over encrypted connections and stored with access controls, so each person can only reach their own information. Our server checks who is signed in on every request. No system is perfectly secure; if a data breach is likely to cause you serious harm, we will tell you and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner, as the law requires.

Your choices and rights

  • See or correct your information: most of it is visible and editable in the app. For a full copy, email us.
  • Delete your account: the bottom of Settings → Delete my account, or email us.
  • Turn things off: you can stop reminders, disconnect a wearable, remove a carer, or clear your companion's memory at any time.

We'll respond to requests within 30 days.

Age

CelerySync is for adults. You must be 18 or over to create an account.

Complaints

If you have a concern about your privacy, please email us first and we'll work with you to fix it. If you're not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at oaic.gov.au or 1300 363 992.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect.
